
# 💰 Bitcoin Miner Tycoon 🚀

**Click your way to crypto riches!** Bitcoin Miner Tycoon is an engaging idle/clicker game where you simulate the thrilling journey of a Bitcoin miner. Start small, build your empire, and dominate the digital currency world!

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

Dive into the world of cryptocurrency mining! You'll start with manual clicks to generate hashes, working your way towards mining your first Bitcoin block. As you accumulate BTC, you can sell it on a dynamic, fluctuating market for dollars. Reinvest your earnings into powerful upgrades to automate your hashing power, boost your efficiency, and watch your crypto fortune grow!

## ✨ Features

*   **⛏️ Click to Mine:** Manually generate hashes with every click. Feel the power!
*   **🤖 Automated Hashing:** Purchase buildings like Mining Rigs, CPU Clusters, and ASICs to generate hashes automatically, even when you're not clicking.
*   **🔋 Power Boosters:** Invest in upgrades like GPU Farms, Cooling Systems, and even Quantum Processors to multiply your total hashing output.
*   **📈 Fluctuating Market:** Experience a simulated Bitcoin market where prices rise and fall. Time your sales strategically to maximize profits!
*   **📅 Time Progression:** Watch the in-game date advance, with the base Bitcoin price evolving over simulated years, mirroring its historical (and fantastical future) trends.
*   **⚖️ Mining Modes:**
    *   **Solo Mining:** Higher risk, higher reward! A chance to mine a larger amount of BTC per block.
    *   **Pool Mining:** Lower, but guaranteed BTC reward per block. Play it safe or go for gold!
*   **⚙️ Game Speed Control:** Accelerate time from 1x up to a blazing 86400x (1 in-game day per second!) to progress faster.
*   **💸 Sell BTC:** Convert your hard-earned Bitcoin into spendable dollars.
*   **🏗️ Building Upgrades:** A wide variety of buildings and research options to enhance your mining operation.
*   **💰 Floating BTC Boosts:** Catch randomly appearing floating "money bags" for a temporary, powerful boost to your hashing speed!
*   **🤖 Auto-Buy Upgrades:** Enable this handy feature to automatically purchase the cheapest available upgrade when you have enough funds.
*   **🔔 Pop-up Notifications:** Stay informed with (toggleable) on-screen alerts for important events like successful mines, purchases, and price changes.
*   **🎨 Sleek Interface:** Enjoy a visually appealing and user-friendly interface with satisfying animations and feedback.
*   **💾 Persistent State (Implicit):** Your progress is maintained as long as the browser tab is open. *(Note: True local storage persistence is not yet implemented in this version).*

## 🚀 How to Play

1.  **Start Hashing:** Click the big "**Click to Generate Hashes**" button. Each click adds to your current hash count.
2.  **Mine Blocks:** Once your "Current Hashes" reach the "Hashes Per Block" target, you'll attempt to mine a block.
    *   Success depends on your chosen **Mining Mode** (Solo or Pool).
3.  **Earn BTC:** Successful blocks reward you with Bitcoin!
4.  **Monitor the Market:** Keep an eye on the "**BTC Price**" and "**Market BTC Price**".
5.  **Sell for Dollars:** Use the "Crypto Exchange" panel to sell your BTC for dollars.
6.  **Buy Upgrades:** Spend your dollars in the "Upgrades & Buildings" section.
    *   **Auto-Hashers** (⛏️, 💻, 💡, etc.) passively generate hashes for you.
    *   **Power Boosters** (🎮, 🌐, ❄️, etc.) increase the percentage output of ALL your hash generators.
7.  **Optimize:**
    *   Switch between **Solo** and **Pool** mining based on your risk appetite.
    *   Adjust **Game Speed** to your preference.
    *   Enable/Disable **Auto-Buy Upgrades** and **Pop-up Notifications** in the "Game Settings".
8.  **Catch Boosts:** Click on the floating 💰 icons that appear on screen for a temporary H/s supercharge!
9.  **Become a Tycoon:** Continue upgrading, selling, and expanding your mining empire!

## 🛠️ Running the Game

1.  Clone this repository or download the files.
2.  Open the `index.html` file in your favorite modern web browser.
3.  Start clicking and have fun!
